## CONTEXT

### Personal Brand Brief

**COLOUR DIRECTION**
Use a restrained technology-oriented colour direction that communicates curiosity, exploration, and building for the future. The main colours should support the content rather than compete with it, allowing projects, ideas, and work to remain the focus.

**Accent:** Use one vibrant accent colour for important actions, highlights, and moments of innovation.

**Why:** The accent should represent energy and curiosity while the overall palette supports a focus on software, intelligent technologies, and building solutions.

**TYPOGRAPHY**
**Heading style:** Bold, confident, and purposeful. Headings should create a strong sense of direction and make the focus on software engineering and AI-powered solutions immediately understandable.

**Body style:** Readable, direct, and approachable. Body text should make ideas and the learning journey easy to follow without sounding overly technical or claiming knowledge that is not supported.

**TONE OF VOICE**
Curious, confident, honest, and driven by learning and building. The writing should show enthusiasm for technology and solving problems while remaining truthful about being an aspiring engineer who is continuously growing.

Example sentence:
*"I build software, explore intelligent technologies, and continuously learn through new challenges."*

**DENSITY**
Airy. Give important statements, projects, and ideas enough space to be understood individually. Use generous spacing between sections and avoid placing too many technologies, claims, or ideas together.

## REQUIREMENTS

* The hero must let a visitor understand what I do within five seconds.
* Clearly establish me as an aspiring Software Engineer who builds software and explores intelligent technologies.
* Projects must be the most visually prominent section on the page.
* The site should communicate curiosity, innovation, versatility, continuous learning, and building.
* Body text must meet a **4.5:1 contrast ratio** against its background.
* The primary call to action must remain reachable at phone width.
* Use airy spacing so important ideas and projects have room to stand on their own.
* Keep the content focused and avoid presenting every technology or interest with equal importance.
* The experience should prioritize evidence of what I build over claims about what I know.

---

## AVOID

This brand should never:

* Claim expertise in every technology.
* Present learning as the same thing as mastery.
* Use exaggerated claims about abilities.
* Hide the focus on building software and solving real-world problems.
* Make curiosity look like a lack of direction.
* Overwhelm visitors by presenting too many technologies or identities equally.
* Sound arrogant or pretend that learning has stopped.
* Use innovation as empty hype without connecting it to building or solving problems.
* Use large gradient backgrounds.
* Use rows of three identical cards.
* Use glass-effect panels.
* Use decorative floating shapes.
* Use oversized headings simply for visual impact.
* Allow certifications or achievements to become more prominent than actual projects.

---

## DESIGN GOAL

Create a portfolio that makes a visitor quickly understand:

> **This is a passionate developer who builds software, explores intelligent technologies, learns continuously, and is serious about creating solutions for the future.**

The portfolio should demonstrate this primarily through **projects and evidence of building**, while supporting the story with education, skills, achievements, and personal motivation.
